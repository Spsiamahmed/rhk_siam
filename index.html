Professional Identity

RHK SIAM (UID: 6979366392) established himself as one of the most consistent performers in the Bangladesh Free Fire circuit. Known for his sharp game sense and calm demeanor under pressure, SIAM was a vital asset for Team RHK (Red Hawks) during their golden era.

Playing alongside esports icons like RHK Kuttush and Nejad, SIAM helped secure the Free Fire Bangladesh Championship (FFBC) 2025 title and became the first Bangladeshi player to compete on the global stage at the Esports World Cup 2025 in Riyadh. Even after RHK's disbandment in April 2026, his legacy continues to inspire upcoming talents.

“RHK gave Bangladesh a new identity in esports. I’m proud to have bled for the Red Hawks.”
